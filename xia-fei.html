APPEARANCES

Xia Fei is a tall and slim young man with a slightly round face, messy black hair dyed as blond and narrow orange eyes with sharp black eyelashes and silver earrings.

Xia Fei's main outfit consists of a black long-sleeved turtleneck shirt, of which the sleeves extend to halfway down his palm. At the top, there is also a silver zipper in the center of it, which extends downwards to the base of his neck. Over this, he wears a dark blue jacket with orange striped and two pockets in the lower half of it. He wears light grey pants that come down to his shoes. His shoes consist of a pair of white sneakers in orange outlines with dark blue soles and laces in red buckles.

As a child, Xia Fei wears a traditional Chinese school uniform.

PERSONALITY

Xia Fei is shown to be an extroverted man who can easily express his thoughts and feelings. He was once an outstanding student—though nowadays he refers to his past self as a nerd. His job at a model agency improved his self-esteem and made him more confident. He is quite sensitive and gets attached to people easily. When he found out about Vein's "death", he promised himself that he'll get revenge for his boss.

When drunk, he can be quite reckless and act without thinking, causing situations where he has to seek Vein's help.

RELATIONSHIP

01
VEIN

Vein is Xia Fei's manager and boss at a modeling agency in Bridon. Vein saved Xia Fei from being drowned to death in a swimming pool by other models. Xia Fei views Vein with a lot of admiration and respect, stating that he's like a big brother to him, and cheering up when Vein came to his rescue. The two appear to be close, with Xia Fei putting a lot of trust in his boss and Vein keeping a close eye on ensuring Xia Fei's diet and actions are in accordance to his principles, on one occasion reprimanding Xia Fei for having bad manners. Vein, for his part, appears to be amused by Xia Fei and has talked about him to Liu Xiao.o.

02
LIU XIAO

Liu Xiao hired Xia Fei to spy on Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang while they are in Bridon. Xia Fei does not seem to like Liu Xiao, as he shows disgust at the idea of being friends, and making his contact name in his phone is "Pain-in-the-Neck Client".

03
CHENG WEIMIN

When Xia Fei was a young child, he knew Cheng Weimin while he was a teacher at Bahati. The extent to their interactions is unknown, but Cheng Weimin may have been a mentor figure to the young Xia Fei

04
CHENG XIAOSHI

Initially, Xia Fei was hired to spy on Cheng Xiaoshi. However, upon meeting him and Lu Guang in Bahati, the Xia Fei and Cheng Xiaoshi got along and bonded over beers, with Xia Fei lamenting over his academic and financial situation and Cheng Xiaoshi attempting to cheer him up. Xia Fei later helped Cheng Xiaoshi find more information about Bahati, resulting in them and Lu Guang doing a modeling job where he began to see them as his friends. This led to Xia Fei feeling conflicted over the fact that he was still being paid to spy on them. Later, once Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang were leaving Bridon, Xia Fei went with them to the airport and saw them off, wishing them the best.
