INTRODUCTION

Vein, full name Xiao Weiying (Chinese: 萧未影 Xiāo Wèiyǐng) is the main antagonist of the Bridon Arc of the Link Click donghua series. He is the head of Chinatown in Bridon, and works as a manager at a modeling agency on the side in Bridon.

APPEARANCES

Vein appears to be a tall and slightly muscular man with a triangular face and red eyes with a red eyeliner hidden by round sunglasses. He has slicked red hair with some bangs covering the forehead and ending up with a braid by a black holder. His outfit consists of burgundy red clover-shaped tassel earrings, a black sweater with a necklance that has a silver moon-shaped necklance and red flower-shaped bracelets under a white hanfu in red outlines in the sleeves and a navy blue coat that is red inside, as well a pair of dark blue pants kept in a black belt with a silver belt holding a similar the same tassel earring and a chain and black shoes.

As a child, vein wears a fully white suit and lacks its sunglasses.

PERSONALITY

From the outside, Vein is characterized as an eccentric but friendly man. He often acts playful, making bizarre remarks like telling Cheng Xiaoshi, "you look tasty". According to Xia Fei, Vein is the kindest person that someone is looking up to. However, this lighthearted demeanor masks Vein into a more sinister side. When his interests are threatened, Vein is revelead to be a psychopath of the worst way imaginable that is capable of suddenly using violence against others to achieve his goals.

Vein has strong principles and dislikes it when others lie or break those principles. On multiple instances, Vein reprimands characters for having bad manners.

STORY

In the original timeline, Vein hunted Lu Guang and Cheng Xiaoshi down for altering the timeline. Vein shot Cheng Xiaoshi, leading to his death and to Lu Guang diving back in time to save him. During the Bridon Arc, Vein worked with Liu Xiao to uncover information about Cheng Weimin and the Bahati fire incident.

Vein runs a modeling agency on the side. In the past, Vein had saved Xia Fei from being drowned while on a modeling job. In the present, Vein appears after Xia Fei calls him and saves Cheng Xiaoshi, Lu Guang, and Xia Fei from being attacked by angry sports fans. He effortlessly fends off the attackers and introduces himself to Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang, telling the former that he "looks tasty". After Lu Guang and Cheng Xiaoshi leave, Xia Fei laments that Vein must have scared them away with his bizarre remarks, implying that Vein had said the same thing to other prospective models.

Later on, Lu Guang and Cheng Xiaoshi get hotpot with Vein. When Cheng Xiaoshi brings up Wang Qing and the Bahati fire, Vein's friendly demeanor is replaced by a cold intensity as he asks them whether they're involved with the incident. Vein quickly puts up a friendly face again and agrees to help them, but only if Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang agree to model for him.

After the modeling gig, Vein offers Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang a modeling contract, but Cheng Xiaoshi declines. Vein reveals that he has arranged for a counseling session for Cheng Xiaoshi with Wang Qing (now "Chris"), who is a psychiatrist. After Cheng Xiaoshi leaves, Vein questions Lu Guang about why he always looks at him with a murderous intent. Vein presents Lu Guang with Wang Qing's psychological hypothetical and laughs at his response, suggesting they share a similar perspective.

Vein receives a call and tells Lu Guang that his friend is in trouble. Vein leaves, claiming he's going to lend them a hand. When Lu Guang calls out his lie, Vein challenges Lu Guang to stop him. Knowing he can't stop him, Lu Guang delays Vein by musing on whether or not he could prevent a fixed node by creating another unchangeable node, to which Vein calls him insane.

ein enters Wang Qing's office and tranquilizes Cheng Xiaoshi. Vein and Shao Yuanyuan (possessing Wang Qing) fight, with Vein getting the upper hand and retrieving Cheng Weimin's notebook. As he leaves, Lu Guang through unknown means causes Wang Qing to regain control and use her powers on Vein, causing him to have a heart attack and collapse. Afterwards, the police arrive and declare Vein dead.An unknown amount of time later, Liu Xiao unzips Vein's body bag and Vein's eyes open.

RELATIONSHIP

01
XIA FEI

Vein is Xia Fei's manager and boss at a modeling agency in Bridon. Vein saved Xia Fei from being drowned to death in a swimming pool by other models. The two appear to be close, with Xia Fei putting a lot of trust in his boss and relying on his guidance. As his manager, Vein manages Xia Fei's behavior and well being, monitoring his diet and correcting his manners in accordance with his standards. Vein has come to Xia Fei's aid more than once, arriving swiftly when he was in trouble. Vein, for his part, appears to be amused by Xia Fei and has talked about him to Liu Xiao. According to an interview with series director Li Haoling, Vein initially hired Xia Fei because he found him entertaining.

02
LIU XIAO

Liu Xiao and Vein appear to be working together in tracking down Lu Guang and Cheng Xiaoshi.

03
LU GUANG

Though the specifics of how Vein views Lu Guang remains unclear, it's evident that Lu Guang harbors deep disdain for Vein for Cheng Xiaoshi's death. Enough so that when the two meet after Lu Guang dives back in time, Vein notices the "murderous intent" in Lu Guang's eyes. Lu Guang is highly cautious of Vein, and their interactions when Cheng Xiaoshi is absent are noteably cold. Vein makes it clear that Lu Guang cannot stop him. In another instance, when Vein presents him Wang Qing's hypothetical he is pleased by Lu Guang's answer, indicating a rare moment of mutual understanding.

04
CHENG XIAOSHI

In the previous timeline, Vein kills Cheng Xiaoshi as a punishment for altering time. After Lu Guang dives back in time, when they first meet in Bridon Vein acts friendly to Cheng Xiaoshi, calling him "tasty". As a result, Cheng Xiaoshi is friendly with Vein and trusts him since Xia Fei trusts him. Vein later helps Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang find information regarding Bahati in exchange for them being his models. At this point, Vein seemingly isn't fully aware of Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang's abilities. Therefore, when recovering Cheng Weimin's notebook, he doesn't see Cheng Xiaoshi has a threat but rather an obstacle and tranquilizes him rather than killing him.
